Publications & Manuscripts

My research combines machine learning, statistical modelling and computational analysis. I work on problems involving noisy data, uncertainty, time-dependent observations and large scientific datasets.

This page contains my published research as well as manuscripts in preparation. Links to the paper, preprint, manuscript and supporting research material will be added to each entry below.

Published

GRB Light-Curve Reconstruction Using Machine-Learning and Probabilistic Methods

Research in machine learning, Gaussian Processes, Deep Gaussian Processes, neural networks and uncertainty-aware reconstruction of GRB X-ray light curves.

This work focuses on reconstructing noisy and irregularly sampled gamma-ray burst X-ray light curves using machine-learning and probabilistic methods. The project involved large-scale data preparation, model development, validation and uncertainty analysis.

Ultra-Fast Outflows in AGN

Large-scale X-ray data analysis, statistical signal detection, spectral modelling and photoionisation modelling.

This project uses automated Python pipelines and archival X-ray observations to investigate high-velocity outflows in active galactic nuclei. The work involves large-scale catalogue cross-matching, data selection, spectral analysis and statistical evaluation of absorption features.

Coronal Temperature and Multi-Epoch X-Ray Spectral Modelling

Multi-epoch data analysis, statistical modelling, parameter estimation and MCMC analysis.

This project investigates the properties of hot X-ray emitting coronae using multi-epoch observations. The analysis combines automated data reduction, broadband spectral modelling, parameter estimation and statistical comparison across observations.
