Constraining the Coronal Geometry in AGN with a Ring-like Corona Model

In this work we have explored the geometry of corona with different relativistic models. This work involves the simultaneous study of XMM-Newton and NuSTAR observations. I have modelled the source while covering all signature such as warm absorbers, ultra-fast outflows, emission, distant reflection and relativistic reflection. We also used MCMC to explore the parameter space. Also performed effect of returning radiation which sees in highly rotating Black Hole (AGN). Then followed by other scenarios such as illumination pattern on accretion disk, coronal geometry and coronal size. The results are consistent with the previous work and also we have constrained the coronal geometry in a ring-like structure. This work is in preparation for publication.
