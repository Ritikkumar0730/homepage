Constraining the Coronal Geometry of IRAS 09149−6206
with a Ring-like Corona Model

We present a broadband X-ray spectral analysis of the Type-I Seyfert galaxy IRAS 09149−6206 (z = 0.0573, dL = 267 Mpc) using simultaneous XMM-Newton and NuSTAR observations covering 0.3–79 keV. We apply the newly introduced relxill_ring model, which extends the conventional lamp-post geometry by representing the X-ray corona as a radially extended ring above the accretion disk, characterised by a spherical source radius rsrc and polar angle θsrc. Accounting for a multi-component warm absorber and a distant neutral reflector, we constrain the corona to rsrc = 4.12+0.23−0.25 rg, placing it in a compact, near-equatorial configuration close to the black hole. We measure a high black-hole spin of a* = 0.90 ± 0.03, a disk inclination of i = 36.7 ± 1.1°, and a large reflection fraction of Rfrac = 2.77, consistent with strong light bending near the black hole. These results are robust with respect to the inclusion of returning radiation and are consistent with prior lamp-post analysis, while demonstrating that the ring geometry provides a physically richer description of the coronal structure in this source.
