Beyond Research

Learning, collaboration, communication, problem solving and interests beyond my academic work.

Beyond Research

Research is an important part of what I do, but I also value the skills and experiences that develop outside a single research problem. I enjoy learning new technical ideas, working with people from different backgrounds and communicating complex concepts clearly.

My academic work has taught me to approach unfamiliar problems independently, ask precise questions and keep working through problems when the solution is not immediately obvious. I try to carry the same approach into everything I learn.

I enjoy environments where people are curious, open to different approaches and willing to challenge an idea constructively. Collaboration and clear communication are as important to me as technical ability.

Learning and Problem Solving

Learning New Ideas

I regularly learn outside the immediate requirements of a project, particularly in mathematics, statistics, machine learning, computational methods and quantitative research.

Independent Work

I enjoy taking ownership of open-ended problems, building a first solution independently and then improving it through testing, discussion and feedback.

Communication

I try to explain technical work in a way that makes the main idea, assumptions and limitations clear to both technical and non-technical audiences.

Collaboration

My research has involved working with researchers from different institutions and backgrounds, which has taught me the value of clear communication, reliability and sharing ideas openly.
